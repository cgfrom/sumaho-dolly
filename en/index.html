Sumaho Dolly

Move your iPhone like a handheld camera, and the camera in Blender on your computer moves the same way. Real handheld camera work, straight into 3D.

Features

Live camera control

Drive the Blender camera from your iPhone’s position and orientation, 60 times per second.

Scale

Choose how far the camera moves when you move your iPhone 1 m (0.1x to 10x), from tabletop miniatures to whole cities.

Smoothing

Tame small hand jitter as much as you like.

Camera View

See Blender’s camera view on your iPhone while you shoot, with your iPhone’s camera feed as an optional overlay.

RecordingPurchase required

Record camera moves as takes in Blender while the timeline plays. Unlimited takes, any length.

Control from your iPhone

Re-center, play back, choose and delete takes, and save your Blender file, all from your iPhone.

Get Started

  1. Install the appGet Sumaho Dolly from the App Store (coming soon).
  2. Install the add-onDownload the .zip above and drag it onto the Blender window.
    If that doesn’t work, go to Edit > Preferences > Add-ons, open the “∨” menu at the top right, choose “Install from Disk…”, and pick the .zip.
  3. Start receivingIn Blender’s 3D Viewport, press N, open the “Sumaho Dolly” tab on the right, and click “Start Receiving”.
  4. ConnectPut your iPhone and computer on the same Wi-Fi network, enter the “This PC’s IP” shown in the tab into the app, and tap “Start Sending”.
  5. MoveHold your iPhone sideways (top on the left) and move it slowly.

On Windows, if a firewall prompt appears the first time you click “Start Receiving”, allow access on private networks.

Requirements

iPhone

Computer
A Mac or Windows PC that runs Blender 4.2 or later

iPhone and computer on the same Wi-Fi network
