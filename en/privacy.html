Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Sumaho Dolly (“the App”) sends the motion of your iPhone to Blender running on a computer on the same local network. This policy describes how the App and the “Sumaho Dolly” add-on for Blender handle information.

1. We do not collect any data

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, or usage data, and does not send any data to the developer’s servers or to third parties. The App contains no advertising and no tracking.

2. Camera

The App uses the camera to track the motion (position and orientation) of your iPhone. Camera images are only shown on your iPhone’s screen. They are never saved or transmitted.

3. Local network

The App sends your iPhone’s motion to the computer (Blender) on your local network that you specify. Nothing is sent to servers on the internet. Your computer sends back a view from the Blender camera and status information. The Blender add-on also communicates only within your local network and never connects to the internet.

4. Data stored on your iPhone

The App stores the IP address and port of your computer, your display settings, and whether you have purchased recording on your iPhone for the next time you use it. They are removed when you delete the App.

5. Recorded motion

Camera motion you record is saved inside your Blender file on your computer. It is not kept in the App.

6. In-app purchase

Recording is purchased through Apple’s App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple and is not received by the developer.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
